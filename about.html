Our Mission & Founder

Start The Actions To Soar (STATS) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering aspiring artists and creative professionals, especially those from underserved and underrepresented communities. Through mentorship, education, creative workforce development, music production, and career pathway opportunities, STATS helps individuals overcome barriers and transform their talents into meaningful futures.

Founded by musician, songwriter, producer, and author Lucius Hoskins, STATS is committed to creating access, exposure, and opportunity for the next generation of creatives. We believe talent is universal, but opportunity is not—and our mission is to ensure that every aspiring artist has the chance to soar.

Together, we are inspiring dreams, building confidence, and creating pathways to success through the arts.
